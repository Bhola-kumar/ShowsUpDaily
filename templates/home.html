ShowsUpDaily

A personal tool for scheduling daily video posts to YouTube.

Sign in to your dashboard

What the app does

ShowsUpDaily helps a channel owner upload and manage videos, choose a daily posting time and visibility, and publish videos to their connected YouTube channel. You can pause automation, review recent activity, and disconnect YouTube from the dashboard.

How it works

  • Sign in to the private dashboard with the password provided by the instance operator.
  • Connect a YouTube channel using Google's authorization flow. The app requests permission to upload videos and read basic channel information.
  • Upload a video, set a schedule, and optionally configure Gemini to generate descriptions and tags from the title and notes you provide.

Videos and settings are stored by the instance operator, either on the server or in its configured storage provider. Google, YouTube, Gemini, and hosting or storage providers are independent services with their own terms and privacy policies. The app does not guarantee successful or uninterrupted publishing.
